# ECSS-Q-ST-80 compliance matrix {#UUID7_DOC_Q80_COMPLIANCE}

<!-- ecss:docinfo id=UUID7-DOC-Q80CM -->

The compliance matrix of the SPAP <8> (ECSS-Q-ST-80 5.2.1.5): every clause of Table D-2 that carries an applicability, its applicability at category D, the compliance (C compliant, PC partially compliant, NY not yet: the activity is planned but has not happened, NC not compliant, NA not applicable), the reference to the project documentation and remarks. A PC or NY row says what is missing and what closes it; the open items are in the \ref UUID7_DOC_SDP "SDP" <4.6>. The data is `ecss/PAF/q80_compliance.csv`.
