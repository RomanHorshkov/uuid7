## 1 Introduction

The design justification of uuid7 in the DJF: the result of every significant trade-off, feasibility analysis and make-or-buy decision of the requirements and architecture process (ECSS-E-ST-40 4.2.4), and the rationale for the important design choices with the analysis and test data that show the design meets its requirements (ECSS-E-ST-40 4.2.5). ECSS gives it no DRD, so its sections follow those two clauses. The SDD summarises each trade-off (SDD <4.7>, item 7, after the DRD's "main design trade-off", ECSS-E-ST-40 Annex F <4.7>) and links here for the full record. It is reviewed at the PDR (the trade-offs of the architecture) and the CDR (the rationale of the detailed design).

Every record is generated from the rms: an artifact of kind decision with its alternatives and consequences, the rationale and assumption artifacts linked to it, the requirements it answers, the components it shapes, and the verification cases that justify it with their result in this refresh. Nothing here is written by hand; the links run both ways (a requirement in the SRS and a component in the SDD name the decisions that concern them).
