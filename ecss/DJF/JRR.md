# Joint review reports {#UUID7_DOC_JRR}

<!-- ecss:docinfo id=UUID7-DOC-JRR -->

The outcome of every review of the \ref UUID7_DOC_SREVP "SRevP" as the rms export records it, generated at every refresh (ECSS-E-ST-40 5.3.3.1a): who closed it, the baseline that froze it and who froze and approved that, the time from opening to closing, the decision, every revision of the review artifact, and its RIDs (rms review-comments `UUID7-RID-<review>-nnn`, SRevP <13>) with who raised and who dispositioned them. Nothing here is written by hand: an actor the export does not carry is shown as unrecorded.
