# ECSS-E-ST-40 compliance matrix {#UUID7_DOC_E40_COMPLIANCE}

<!-- ecss:docinfo id=UUID7-DOC-E40CM -->

The coverage matrix of the SDP <5.6> (ECSS-E-ST-40 5.3.9): every requirement identification of Annex R Table R-1 (after the informative clauses of chapter 4 the package cites), its applicability at category D, the compliance (C compliant, PC partially compliant, NY not yet: the activity is planned but has not happened, NC not compliant, NA not applicable) and where the expected output is. A PC or NY row says what is missing and what closes it; the open items are in the \ref UUID7_DOC_SDP "SDP" <4.6>. The disposition beyond Annex R is justified in \ref UUID7_DOC_TAILORING "the tailoring". The data is `ecss/MGT/e40_compliance.csv`.
