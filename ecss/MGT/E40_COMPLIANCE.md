# ECSS-E-ST-40 compliance matrix {#UUID7_DOC_E40_COMPLIANCE}

<!-- ecss:docinfo id=UUID7-DOC-E40CM -->

The coverage matrix of the SDP <5.6> (ECSS-E-ST-40 5.3.9): every requirement identification of Annex R Table R-1, its applicability at category D, the compliance (C compliant, NC not compliant, NA not applicable) and where the expected output is. The disposition beyond Annex R is justified in \ref UUID7_DOC_TAILORING "the tailoring". The data is `ecss/MGT/e40_compliance.csv`.
