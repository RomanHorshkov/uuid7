## 1 Introduction

This SRevP defines the reviews of uuid7: what each one decides, which documents it needs, how it runs and how its outcome is recorded in the rms (ECSS-E-ST-40 5.3.3.2b). One plan covers all the reviews of the life cycle; sections <4> to <13> apply to each review, with its own objectives in <6> and its own data package in <10>.

## 4 Review title and project

### 4.1 Exact name

| Review | Exact name | Combined with |
|---|---|---|
| SRR | uuid7 software requirements review | SWRR and PDR (one meeting, three decisions) |
| PDR | uuid7 preliminary design review | SRR, SWRR |
| CDR | uuid7 critical design review | DDR, software validation specification review |
| QR | uuid7 qualification review | software delivery review board |
| AR | uuid7 acceptance review | software delivery review board |

No ORR: the library has no operation of its own (\ref UUID7_DOC_TAILORING "tailoring").

### 4.2 System or product subject to review

The uuid7 library (libuuid7, libuuid7-dev) at the commit proposed for the next release tag, with its qualification package regenerated on that commit.

## 5 Reference documents

The project documentation applicable to every review: the \ref UUID7_DOC_SDP "SDP", this plan, the \ref UUID7_DOC_SPAP "SPAP" and \ref UUID7_DOC_TAILORING "the tailoring". The review data package of each review is the DRL of ECSS-E-ST-40 Table A-1 for that review, listed in <10>.

## 6 Review objectives

- SRR (with SWRR and PDR): agree that the requirements of the TS capture what the library must do, given its upper-level sources (no RB); release the TS (SRS, ICD); check the plans (SDP, SRevP, SCMP, SPAP, SVerP, SValP, SSMP) and the security analysis; verify the technical budgets' estimates; evaluate the reuse file; release the architecture (SDD); evaluate known issues; decide to proceed.
- CDR (with DDR and SVSR): baseline the detailed design (SDD, code, doc comments); check the unit test plan and its results; check the validation specification against the TS and baseline it; verify the coverage, the technical budgets and the reuse file; release the SUM; check that the configuration item is a formal version; evaluate known issues; decide to proceed.
- QR: verify that the library meets every TS requirement (validation and verification complete, the verification and traceability matrices, coverage); release the SRelD; verify the maintenance plan; check the delivery (packages, checksums, SCF); decide to deliver.
- AR: verify that the delivered packages are the qualified ones; accept them for pinning by the consumers; evaluate the known problems; close the delivery.

Security is an agenda item of every review: the SSAR and its treatment status (ECSS-E-ST-40 5.3.3.1b).

## 7 Expected results

1. Entry criteria: the qualification package regenerated by `ecss/make.sh` on the commit under review, pipeline green, no uncommitted change; the rms export of that commit present under `ecss/rms_export/`.
2. Success criteria: the objectives of <6> met; every action and RID due before the review closed; the RIDs of the review dispositioned and their actions assigned.
3. Conclusion: successful, successful with rework (open change requests with dates), or not successful (no baseline).
4. Report: the rms review artifact (status approved or needs-rework), its review-comments (RIDs) and the baseline the review freezes; the \ref UUID7_DOC_JRR "joint review reports" are generated from them.

## 8 Review process

1. Planning: the review artifact is created in the rms (status proposed) when a release is prepared.
2. Participants: the maintainer; a consumer's maintainer is invited when the interface changes.
3. Kick-off: none separate; the data package is the site of `ecss/make.sh`.
4. Data package readiness check: the entry criteria of <7>.
5. Presentation: optional.
6. Distribution: the site and the rms export.
7. Study and RIDs: every document is read against its DRD and its checklist (\ref UUID7_DOC_SVERP "SVerP" <6>); each finding is an rms review-comment linked to the reviewed document (`reviews`).
8. Disposition of RIDs: accepted (change request), rejected (reason recorded).
9. Meeting: the maintainer's decision, recorded on the review artifact.
10. Actions closure: change requests implemented and verified, the package regenerated.
11. Closure: `kyr-rms-cli baseline <REVIEW>` freezes every artifact at its current revision; the review artifact goes to approved.

Agenda of a presentation when held: the review group, the context and requirements, the product (definition, critical points, performance), the previous review's actions.

## 9 Review schedule

Data package delivery (the regenerated site) → study and RIDs (one working day) → disposition → decision → baseline, all before the release tag. Dates are those of the rms review artifact and baseline.

## 11 Participants

1. Decision making authority: the maintainer, Roman Horshkov.

## 13 Annex — RID form

A RID is an rms artifact of kind `review-comment`:

| Field | rms field |
|---|---|
| RID identifier | human_id `UUID7-RID-<review>-nnn` |
| Reviewed item | link `reviews` to the document or requirement |
| Discrepancy | title and body |
| Proposed solution | rationale |
| Disposition | status (proposed, approved = accepted, rejected) and change reason |
| Action | link `impacts` from the change request that implements it |
